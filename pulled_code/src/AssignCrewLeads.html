👷 Assign Crew Leads

Click an employee to assign them as crew lead for their job. This designation is separate from Job Classification.

ℹ️ What is a Crew Lead? A Crew Lead is the person responsible for their crew's tasks in the Task List and Trip Planner. This is independent of their Job Classification (SUP, GF, F, etc.). Employees with lead classifications (SUP, GF, F, GTO F) are automatically considered crew leads unless you manually assign someone else.
