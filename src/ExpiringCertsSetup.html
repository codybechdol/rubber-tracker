Certification Categories & Behavior Rules

🟢 Standard Auto-Term

Has fixed duration (e.g. 12, 24, 36, 60 mos). Selecting Date Acquired automatically calculates the Expiration Date.

🟡 Variable / Manual

Expiration varies by doctor or state (e.g. MEC, DL). Entering Date Acquired requires specifying an Expiration Date.

🔵 Non-Expiring (Lifetime / Eval)

One-time evaluation or lifetime cert (e.g. Crane Eval, OSHA 1910). Only Date Issued / Performed is tracked.
